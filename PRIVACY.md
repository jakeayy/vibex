# VibeX Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-27_

VibeX is a browser extension that customizes the X (x.com / twitter.com) web
interface. This policy explains what it does with your data. The short version:
**VibeX does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your data.**

## Data collection

VibeX collects **no** user data. It does not gather, record, or transmit any
personally identifiable information, authentication details, browsing history,
location, communications, or any other personal data. VibeX has no servers,
analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind, and sends nothing to the
developer or to any third party.

## How VibeX works

VibeX runs entirely on your own device, only on x.com and twitter.com:

- **Settings** you choose are saved in your browser's local storage on your own
  device. They never leave your browser and are not synced to any server.
- VibeX reads and modifies the X page you are viewing in order to apply the
  features you enable (for example hiding elements or changing the interface).
  This processing happens locally in your browser and is never transmitted
  anywhere.
- The optional request-blocking feature **blocks** certain network requests
  (such as ads, tracking, or Grok endpoints) according to your settings. It
  does not read, record, or send the contents of your requests.
- The optional media-download feature saves a file to your device when you
  choose to download it. Nothing is uploaded.

## Permissions

- **Host access to x.com and twitter.com** is required so VibeX can run on those
  pages and apply your chosen changes. VibeX requests no access to any other
  site.
- **declarativeNetRequest** is used only to block requests you have opted to
  block; the blocking rules are bundled with the extension and disabled by
  default.

## Third parties

VibeX does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect any
data to share.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same
location with a new "Last updated" date.
